How it works

Three steps from idea to export.

  1. 01

    Describe it

    Tell the creative director what you're launching: the name, what it does, two or three benefits and a call to action. Paste a Pinterest, Behance or product link for references.

  2. 02

    Add your voice-over

    Upload a 30–60 second voice-over. Hookky finds the natural pauses, aligns the words, and builds scenes that cut where you breathe, with kinetic type that follows the speech.

  3. 03

    Refine and export

    Drag scenes, snap cuts to pauses, swap styles, and let the director's notes catch what's too long or too fast. Export frame-accurate MP4s in every format at once.
