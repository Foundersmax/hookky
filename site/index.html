How it works

From brief to export in three steps.

  1. 01

    Brief the director

    Name the product, its benefits and the call to action. Pick one of three creative directions it pitches back.

  2. 02

    Add your voice

    Upload a 30–60 second voice-over. Scenes cut on its pauses and the type follows the words.

  3. 03

    Refine and export

    Edit on the canvas, add stock video, then export MP4, GIF, captions and posters in every format.
